ZHSan, or Zhonghua Sanguozhi (中华三国志), is another project team’s game and brand. It is not a Season-owned product, an edition of SanguoSeason, or the new Sanguo3D project.

This site introduces the SeasonRealms/ZHSan branch as a friendly, independently maintained companion. Its place here is a link from the home page, not one of the site’s four main product columns.

This is not the official Zhonghua Sanguozhi website. We do not speak for the original team or its community.

When looking for information or reporting an issue, identify the branch you are using. A change in SeasonRealms/ZHSan does not necessarily describe the original project.
